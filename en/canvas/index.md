## Dynamics & Methodology

Enterprise AI pilots fail when they start with model selection without separating deterministic rules from probabilistic inference. The canvas structures ten blocks across three contiguous zones of executive decision-making.

### Business Diagnostics

Block 1 occupies one quarter of the board to dissect the core process bottleneck or the senior specialist's scope. Block 2 isolates pure deterministic code from AI reasoning, and Block 3 anticipates internal political friction.

### Ontology & Governance

The agent never runs raw SQL against production databases. It interacts exclusively with the Operational Ontology via typed objects and actions, operating under strict human-in-the-loop thresholds and OpenTelemetry tracing.

### P&L Anchoring & Scale

Replaces vague productivity promises with auditable Dual Bleed math across operational hours and idle software licenses, establishing an objective Day-5 acceptance gate to unlock production rollout.
