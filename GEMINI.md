# Gemini Agent Directives — HSN Labs Official Website

Operational directives for Gemini models maintaining, extending, or refactoring this repository.

## 1. Mission & Core Positioning

HSN Labs is a **Forward Deployed Engineering boutique** building custom, deterministic multi-agent architectures on executable business ontologies for mission-critical enterprise operations.

* **Primary Language:** The website must be **100% in English**. All copy, labels, meta tags, and alt texts are strictly English.
* **Target Audience:** Founders, C-levels (CTO, CIO, CFO), and engineering directors in mid-to-large enterprises with high data volume, complex compliance, and margin exposure.
* **Aesthetic Standard:** Strict engineering discipline (Resend / Linear dark engineering UI). Rejects all AI slop: no generic neon purple gradients, no floating data ribbons, no abstract robotic illustrations.

## 2. Structural Architecture of the Website

The website follows a validated 4-section architecture:

1. **Header:**
   - Fixed, translucent dark navbar (`rgba(7, 9, 14, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
   - Left: Official HSN Labs horizontal lockup (`hsn-labs` SVG + symbol).
   - Navigation links: Services, Verticals, Methodology, Contact.
   - CTA button: Primary button linking to Contact / Bootcamp application.
2. **Hero Page:**
   - Technical eyebrow tag: `Forward Deployed Engineering • Mission-Critical Systems`.
   - Primary headline: High-impact statement of deterministic agentic systems.
   - Subtitle: Narrative explaining senior embedded FDE squads versus theoretical management consulting slides.
   - Interactive Visual Centerpiece: The official HSN Labs 3-stage water motion logo.
   - Enterprise Portfolio Strip: Normalized monochrome vector logos of client enterprises: Deloitte, PagoNext, Insi, Softplan, Unipar, LWSA, Turbi, Caju, Cast Group.
3. **Services Section (Our Delivery Model):**
   - 4 technical pillars:
     1. Embedded FDE Squads (Senior engineers embedded inside client VPC).
     2. Deterministic Guardrails (Invariant enforcement and schema validation eliminating stochastic drift).
     3. Business Ontologies & GraphRAG (Executable knowledge graphs bridging legacy ERPs and multi-agent systems).
     4. 5-Day Agentic Bootcamp (Rapid-validation deployment delivering a verified functioning agent on client data).
4. **Verticals Section (Markets We Serve):**
   - Dedicated cards for regulated enterprise industries:
     1. BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, Insurance) — Underwriting, reconciliation, fraud/compliance.
     2. Healthcare — Clinical auditing, billing workflows, HIPAA/privacy VPC isolation.
     3. Enterprise Operations & Logistics — Supply chain telemetry, procurement routing, ERP integration.
5. **Contact Us Section:**
   - Direct technical scoping interface for executive sponsors.
   - Requirements checklist: scale, urgency, data access, executive sponsor.
6. **Footer:**
   - Institutional signature, legal notice, Proto-Ontologies link, and `llms.txt` AI crawler index link.

## 3. Design Tokens & Styling Rules

* **Design Tokens:** Follow `DESIGN.md`, `tokens.json`, and `tailwind.theme.json` strictly.
* **Color Palette:**
  - Canvas: `#07090E` (Obsidian Black)
  - Surface: `#0E1017` / `#11141D`
  - Border: `1px solid #222736` (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)
  - Primary Accent: `#52B4FD` (Sky Blue water)
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF`
  - Text Secondary: `#94A3B8`
  - Text Muted: `#718096`
* **Typography:**
  - Headings: Satoshi 700 / 900 bold (or Inter Display)
  - Body: Inter regular 400 and medium 500
  - Telemetry & Code: JetBrains Mono 500
* **Logo Application:** Never alter the origami carp proportions or angle. Keep the carp static and tactile while animating the liquid cyan water medium.
